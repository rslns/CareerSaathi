# CareerSaathi AI

> A context-aware AI career and entrepreneurship guide built to help students turn uncertainty into an actionable roadmap.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://careersaathi.vercel.app/

---

## The Problem

Career advice is often generic.

A student may ask:

> "What should I do after my degree?"

But the useful answer depends on much more than the question itself—education, interests, location, goals, preferences, available opportunities, and constraints all matter.

CareerSaathi was built around a simple idea:

**Career guidance should understand the person before recommending the path.**

---

## What I Built

CareerSaathi is an AI-powered platform that collects a user's context and uses it to generate personalized career and entrepreneurship guidance.

Instead of presenting a fixed list of careers, the application creates recommendations based on the individual's profile.

It can help users explore:

- Career opportunities
- Entrepreneurship ideas
- Business directions
- Government schemes
- Networking opportunities
- Skill and career roadmaps
- Personalized next steps

The application also supports multiple languages to make the guidance more accessible.

---

## How AI Fits Into the Product

AI is not an isolated chatbot feature in CareerSaathi.

It is used as the reasoning and generation layer behind the personalized recommendations.
